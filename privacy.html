Privacy Policy

Cosmo's Card Hop, a free game for Cosmic Collectables & Games. Last updated October 13, 2026.

What we collect

If you post a score, we save the name you type, your score, distance, number of cards, how long the run lasted, and the time it was posted. That is all. You do not need to sign in or give an email address.

Please do not put personal information, such as your real full name, in the leaderboard name box. Names are shown publicly on the Top Pilots list.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps your best score and the last name you used, so the game can show them next time. You can clear this in your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not run analytics.

Discord

When the game runs as a Discord Activity, it talks to Discord only so it can open and display inside Discord and open links. We do not receive or store your Discord name, ID or messages.

Where it is stored

Scores are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. The game page is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services process basic technical data, like your IP address, to deliver the page, under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Leaderboard names and scores are public by design.

Removing your score

To have a name or score removed, message the store on Facebook or Instagram with the name used and we will remove it.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and does not ask for personal details. Please do not enter personal information in the name box.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
